KR Stream Clipper

KR Stream Clipper is a private tool operated by a single person. It creates short vertical video clips from Grand Theft Auto VI livestreams of popular streamers, and uploads them as drafts to the operator's own TikTok account via the official TikTok Content Posting API. Every draft is reviewed, captioned and published manually by the operator in the TikTok app.

The tool is not offered to other users. No accounts other than the operator's own TikTok account are connected.
