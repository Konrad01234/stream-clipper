2. What Stream Clipper does

Stream Clipper is a private tool used only by the controller. It runs on the controller's own computer, creates short clips from public Grand Theft Auto VI livestream recordings of popular streamers, and uploads them as drafts to the controller's own TikTok account.

3. Data processed via TikTok

When the controller connects their TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the tool receives an access token and a refresh token for the scope video.upload, plus basic account information returned by TikTok (open ID). These are used only to upload video drafts to that account and to check the upload status. Tokens are stored locally on the controller's computer and are never shared.

4. Data of other people

The tool processes publicly available stream recordings, public chat activity counts and public clip metadata to find interesting moments. Chat messages are only counted and not stored permanently; no profiles of chat users are created. Source recordings are deleted after processing; finished clips are deleted after 14 days.

5. Recipients

TikTok (upload of drafts). Telegram (notifications to the controller only). No data is sold or shared with any other party.
